Residential security services

Every package is built around your property and can be scaled up or down as your needs change.

24-Hour Security Patrols

Our existing 24hr patrol service may already operate in your area. Join one of our patrol routes and recommend a neighbour to make significant savings. Highly visible patrol vehicles, equipped with the latest GPS tracking, act as a proactive deterrent — with packages for individuals or groups of local residents. All staff are highly trained, vetted to BS7858, licensed by the SIA and hold enhanced DBS checks.

Security Concerns

Subscribe to one of our packages and we'll provide a free security assessment of your property. Concerned about a security issue right now? We can deploy a response unit to attend promptly.

Alarm Response

If we receive a notification from your alarm monitoring company, our nearest response unit attends within minutes, carries out a dynamic risk assessment, and secures your property. On completion, you'll receive an electronic report with GPS proof of attendance.

Key Holding

Lost your keys or locked out? We're just a phone call away. All customer keys are uniquely tagged and coded, stored in a highly secure vault using the latest Keycafe technology — with a fast response guaranteed within strict timeframes.

Additional Services

A tailor-made service for when you're away from home — short term or long term, on holiday or working away. Upon your instructions, and for added peace of mind, we can patrol your property, feed your pets, collect your doorstep post, water your plants, grant contractor or tradesmen access, and put the bins out.
